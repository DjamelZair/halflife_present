Initiation of lenacapavir with dose escalation in a patient receiving rifabutin: A case report with therapeutic drug monitoring.

Lenacapavir is a first-in-class HIV-1 capsid inhibitor with long-acting pharmacokinetics that enable 6-monthly subcutaneous dosing after initiation with oral lead-in. Lenacapavir is a CYP3A4, UGT1A1 and P-gp substrate; inducers of these enzymes and P-gp can significantly decrease lenacapavir concentrations.
