SGLT2 inhibitor-induced genitourinary infections and the consequent antimicrobial-antidiabetic drug interactions: A narrative review of clinical and pharmacovigilance concern.

Sodium glucose cotransporter 2 (SGLT2) inhibitors provide cardiovascular and renal benefits in type 2 diabetes mellitus, chronic kidney disease, and heart failure, but their use is associated with genitourinary (GU) infections and medication-safety concerns during antimicrobial treatment. This narrative review integrate evidence regarding SGLT2 inhibitor-associated GU infections, antimicrobial-antidiabetic drug interactions, pharmacovigilance, clinical management, and antimicrobial stewardship.
