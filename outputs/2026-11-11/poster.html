Mechanistic insights into vincristine interactions with azoles and dasatinib: Implications for neurotoxicity in paediatric patients using physiologically based pharmacokinetic modelling.

This study aimed to investigate the mechanisms and pharmacokinetic consequences of vincristine (VCR) drug-drug interactions (DDIs) with azole antifungals and dasatinib (DAS). In vitro enzyme and transporter assays were conducted to characterize VCR metabolism and inhibition by posaconazole (POS), voriconazole (VRZ), fluconazole (FLZ) and DAS.

02

Population pharmacokinetics and model-informed precision dosing of busulfan in Chinese pediatric allogeneic hematopoietic stem cell transplantation: a prospective real-world cohort study.

Busulfan is a cornerstone of conditioning regimens for pediatric allogeneic hematopoietic stem cell transplantation (HSCT), yet its narrow therapeutic index and high pharmacokinetic variability necessitate individualized dosing. This study aimed to develop and evaluate a population pharmacokinetic (PopPK) model for busulfan in Chinese pediatric allogeneic HSCT recipients, characterize the exposure-response relationship, and establish a model-informed precision dosing (MIPD) strategy for clinical implementation.

Study to Compare Bictegravir/Lenacapavir Versus Current Therapy in People With HIV-1 Who Are Successfully Treated With a Complicated Regimen (ARTISTRY-1)
