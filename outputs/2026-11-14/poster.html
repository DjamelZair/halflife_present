Interaction of the day: sevabertinib and erythromycin

  • erythromycin is a moderate inhibitor of CYP3A and sevabertinib is a moderate sensitive substrate of CYP3A, so sevabertinib exposure can be higher.

Illustrative AUC ratio 2.4x (moderate increase), using textbook values that reproduce the FDA category definitions, not values for these exact drugs.
