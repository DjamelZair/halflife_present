Prediction of Drug Metabolism-Mediated Clearance and Drug-Drug Interactions.

Drug metabolism-mediated clearance and drug-drug interactions (DDIs) are critical considerations in drug development and therapeutic management. This chapter explores the integration of in vitro systems with physiologically based pharmacokinetic (PBPK) modeling to predict clearance and interaction risk for small-molecule drugs.

Pharmacokinetics, Pharmacodynamics, and Tolerability of BC0305, a Dual SGLT1/2 Inhibitor, in a First-in-Human Single Ascending Dose Study.

BC0305 is a novel dual sodium-glucose cotransporter 1/2 (SGLT1/2) inhibitor in clinical development for the treatment of type 2 diabetes. This first-in-human studyevaluated the safety, pharmacokinetics (PK), and pharmacodynamics (PD) of BC0305 after single ascending oral doses in healthy volunteers.

BC0305 increases urinary glucose excretion in a dose-dependent manner up to 100 mg, suggesting renal SGLT2 inhibition

Exposure increases with dose across the range studied, with a terminal half-life of 8.2-14.6 h

Axillary Reverse Mapping in Preventing Lymphedema in Patients With Breast Cancer Undergoing Axillary Lymph Node Dissection

This phase III trial studies how well axillary reverse mapping works in preventing lymphedema in patients with breast cancer undergoing axillary lymph node dissection. Axillary reverse mapping may help to preserve the lymph node drainage system around the breast so as to prevent lymphedema after surgery.

This study investigates if axillary reverse mapping can help reduce the risk of lymphedema in breast cancer patients undergoing axillary lymph node dissection.
