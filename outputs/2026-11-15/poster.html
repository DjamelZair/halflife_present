Antimicrobial stewardship in critical care: Applying PK/PD and optimised antimicrobial dosing to improve care.

Life-threatening infections are common in critically ill patients and remain a major cause of morbidity and mortality in the intensive care unit (ICU). Antimicrobial stewardship (AMS) aims to optimise antimicrobial use by balancing effective treatment with minimising toxicity, resistance and unnecessary exposure.

Personalized Geriatric Pharmacotherapy: Pharmacogenomics, Polypharmacy, and Age-Related Pharmacokinetic and Pharmacodynamic Changes.

Aging induces profound physiological changes that alter drug disposition and response, narrowing therapeutic windows and increasing susceptibility to adverse drug reactions (ADRs). This review synthesizes current evidence on age-related pharmacokinetic and pharmacodynamic changes, with a focus on pharmacogenomic determinants of drug response in older adults.

Interaction of the day: entrectinib and etravirine

  • etravirine is a moderate inducer of CYP3A and entrectinib is a sensitive substrate of CYP3A, so entrectinib exposure can be lower.

Illustrative AUC ratio 0.36x (moderate decrease), using textbook values that reproduce the FDA category definitions, not values for these exact drugs.
