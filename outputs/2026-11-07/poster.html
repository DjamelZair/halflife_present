Potential drug-drug interaction risks of selective serotonin reuptake inhibitors in outpatients: a retrospective analysis using two screening databases.

Potential drug-drug interactions (pDDIs) involving selective serotonin reuptake inhibitors (SSRIs) pose significant risks in psychiatric care, where polypharmacy often exacerbates pharmacokinetic and pharmacodynamic effects through cytochrome P450 enzyme pathways. This retrospective study aimed to assess the prevalence, characteristics, and risk factors of pDDIs associated with SSRIs in outpatients.

Postmortem femoral venous blood concentrations, redistribution and toxicity of duloxetine.

Duloxetine, a serotonin-norepinephrine reuptake inhibitor (SNRI), is widely prescribed for depression, anxiety, neuropathic pain, and other chronic pain conditions. Its pharmacokinetics show substantial interindividual variability due to sex, age, smoking, and CYP2D6 metabolism.

03 Discovery

A Phase 3 Trial of Epcoritamab vs Investigator's Choice Chemotherapy in Relapsed/Refractory (R/R) Diffuse Large B-cell Lymphoma (DLBCL)

The purpose of this trial is to find out if epcoritamab, also known as EPKINLY™ and GEN3013, is safe and works well as treatment for participants with DLBCL that are not responding to treatment, have grown in size, or have come back following treatment with at least 1 prior systemic cancer therapy. All participants in this trial will be randomly assigned to receive either epcoritamab or a pre-specified investigator's choice (standard of care) chemotherapy (either rituximab + gemcitabine + oxaliplatin \[R-GemOx\], or bendamustine + rituximab \[BR\]).
