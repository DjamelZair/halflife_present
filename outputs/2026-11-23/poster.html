Vancomycin Clearance During CVVHDF With an oXiris Membrane: Pharmacokinetic Evidence of Adsorptive Drug Removal.

Adsorptive hemofilters such as oXiris are increasingly used during continuous renal replacement therapy (CRRT) in septic shock, yet their interaction with antimicrobial agents remains insufficiently characterized. Vancomycin dosing during CRRT is particularly challenging because of altered volume of distribution, protein binding, and extracorporeal clearance.

In vivo study shows time-dependent vancomycin adsorption by oXiris membrane during CVVHDF, affecting drug exposure.

Estimated extracorporeal clearance of vancomycin was approximately 1.25 L/h after first-order correction.

Two-sample machine-learning estimation of rifampicin exposure in tuberculosis: an external validation study.

Subtherapeutic rifampicin exposure drives treatment failure and acquired resistance in tuberculosis. We aimed to develop and externally validate a concentration-driven machine learning (ML) model predicting rifampicin systemic exposure (AUC0-24h) from sparse clinical sampling, addressing the structural biases and limited generalizability of conventional Bayesian precision dosing.

A machine learning model predicts rifampicin systemic exposure (AUC0-24h) from sparse clinical sampling, outperforming traditional methods.

The model was evaluated based on a two-point sampling strategy at 2 and 8 hours post-dose.
