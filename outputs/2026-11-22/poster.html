Development of a machine learning-based treatment-response prediction model for levetiracetam in pediatric epilepsy: integrating ABCB1 polymorphism and therapeutic drug monitoring.

Empirical dosing of levetiracetam (LEV) in pediatric epilepsy often yields variable outcomes. This study aimed to develop a machine learning-based treatment-response prediction model integrating therapeutic drug monitoring (TDM) and ABCB1 C3435 T polymorphism.

The study found that LEV trough concentration and ABCB1 genotype are the strongest associative factors for clinical outcomes.

The XGBoost model demonstrated a high predictive performance, with LEV trough concentration as a key factor.

Attainment of Prophylactic Target Posaconazole Concentrations During Admission for Allogeneic Stem Cell Transplant.

Invasive fungal infections (IFIs) contribute to morbidity and mortality following allogeneic hematopoietic stem cell transplantation (HSCT). Posaconazole is oftentimes utilized for antifungal prophylaxis due to its broad-spectrum activity against yeast and mold.

32.4% of patients achieved initial prophylactic target trough concentration, while 67.6% had concentrations below target.

Median time to target attainment was 16 days (IQR, 7-20 days)

Efficacy and Safety of Benralizumab in Moderate to Very Severe Chronic Obstructive Pulmonary Disease (COPD) With a History of Frequent Exacerbations

Phase 3 study to evaluate the efficacy and safety of a benralizumab in patients with moderate to very severe COPD with a history of frequent COPD exacerbations and elevated peripheral blood eosinophils (≥300/μL). Eligible patients must have a history of ≥2 moderate and/or severe COPD exacerbations in the previous year despite receiving triple (ICS/LABA/LAMA) background therapy for at least 3 months and ICS-based dual inhaled treatment for the remainder of the year.

Study evaluates benralizumab's effectiveness and safety for moderate to severe COPD patients with frequent exacerbations and high eosinophil count.

Study duration varies, primary endpoint analyzed at Week 56.
