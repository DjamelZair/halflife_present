An International Prospective Open-label, Randomized, Phase III Study Comparing 177Lu-PSMA-617 in Combination With Standard of Care (SoC), Versus SoC Alone, in Adult Male Patients With Metastatic Hormone Sensitive Prostate Cancer (mHSPC)

The purpose of this study is to evaluate the efficacy and safety of 177Lu-PSMA-617 in combination with Standard of Care (SOC), versus Standard of Care alone, in adult male patients with Metastatic hormone sensitive prostate cancer (mHSPC).
