SCY-247, a second-generation triterpenoid antifungal, shows low risk for clinically meaningful interactions with CYP3A4 substrates: results from a phase 1 midazolam drug-drug interaction study.

SCY-247, an oral and intravenous second-generation triterpenoid antifungal, is being developed for treatment and prevention of invasive fungal infections caused by Candida, Aspergillus, and Pneumocystis species. In vitro studies indicated that SCY-247 is metabolized by cytochrome P450 (CYP) 3A4 and 3A5 and that SCY-247 has a lowered potential for CYP450-mediated drug-drug interactions compared with azoles.

02

Assessing kidney function for drug dosing in critical illness.

Kidney-function assessment supports drug dosing in critical illness but does not directly measure drug clearance. Reduced creatinine generation, fluid shifts and rapidly changing filtration can make creatinine-based estimates misleading, whereas genuinely augmented renal clearance can cause insufficient drug exposure.
