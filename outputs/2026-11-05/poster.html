Biomonitoring of Oxycodone and Its Metabolites in Conventional and Nonconventional Biological Matrices: A Systematic Critical Review.

Oxycodone is widely prescribed for moderate-to-severe pain, but is associated with a high risk of misuse and overdose. Global data indicate an increase in opioid-related hospitalizations and fatalities, highlighting the need for effective therapeutic drug monitoring (TDM).

Integrated experimental and computational study of a novel indenonaphthodiazepine[5.3.3]propellane as a potential inhibitor of Mcl-1.

Myeloid cell leukemia-1 (Mcl-1) is a key anti-apoptotic protein that drives resistance to chemotherapy, making it a critical oncology target. In this study, we synthesized a new indenonaphthodiazepine[5.3.3]propellane via a one-pot multicomponent reaction and characterized its structure by FT-IR, 1H-NMR, 13C-NMR, and mass spectrometry.

An Extension Study to Evaluate the Long-term Safety and Efficacy of Pegcetacoplan (APL-2) in Subjects With Geographic Atrophy Secondary to AMD

This is a phase 3, open-label, multicenter, extension study to evaluate the long-term safety and efficacy of pegcetacoplan (APL-2) in subjects with geographic atrophy (GA) secondary to age-related macular degeneration (AMD) who participated in Study APL2-103 (NCT03777332) or completed the treatment at Month 24 of either Study APL2-303 (Derby, NCT03525613) or Study APL2-304 (Oaks, NCT03525600).
