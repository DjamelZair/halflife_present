The pharmacotherapeutic use of clozapine in older adults: A narrative review.

Clozapine remains the gold-standard treatment for refractory schizophrenia, yet its use in older adults is often limited by concerns regarding tolerability, age-related pharmacokinetic changes, and the burden of medical comorbidities. This narrative review synthesises current evidence on clozapine use in older populations, with a focus on pharmacokinetic alterations, dosing strategies, comorbidity-related considerations, and clozapine's therapeutic benefits, safety, and risks.

Aging affects clozapine plasma concentrations, increasing susceptibility to adverse effects.

Aging leads to increased clozapine and norclozapine plasma concentrations.

Optimising posaconazole dosing in paediatric patients: a population pharmacokinetic modelling and simulation study.

Posaconazole is approved for prophylaxis and treatment of invasive fungal infections in paediatric patients, but data for optimal formulation-specific dosing across the paediatric weight range remains scant. We pooled individual-level pharmacokinetic data to identify dosing regimens that optimise the probability of achieving predefined efficacy and safety targets.

Study identifies suboptimal oral suspension and suggests loading dose for intravenous and delayed-release tablet formulations

Bioavailability varies with dose, proton pump inhibitor use, and diarrhoea; trough concentration targets not consistently met with current dosing regimens
