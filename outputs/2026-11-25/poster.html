HIV Pre-Exposure Prophylaxis in Solid Organ Transplant Recipients: An Overlooked Component of Long-Term Care.

Sexual health and HIV prevention remain inconsistently addressed during follow-up after solid organ transplantation. This narrative review examines the potential role of HIV pre-exposure prophylaxis (PrEP) in transplant recipients with ongoing or anticipated HIV exposure.

No clinical trials have evaluated PrEP in solid organ transplant recipients, but existing evidence supports individualized consideration.

Tenofovir alafenamide/emtricitabine offers a more favorable renal profile, while long-acting cabotegravir avoids tenofovir exposure.

Hepatocyte-based models for cytochrome P450 fraction metabolized determination: Recommendations from the Quantitative Fraction Metabolized Working Group of the International Consortium for Innovation and Quality in Pharmaceutical Development.

Accurate quantification of fraction metabolized (fm) by drug metabolizing enzymes is essential for understanding clearance mechanisms, predicting interindividual variability, and assessing victim drug-drug interaction (DDI) risk during drug discovery and development. Particularly, determining fm for metabolically stable, low turnover compounds remains challenging.

Study highlights differences in intrinsic clearance, inhibitor response, and fraction metabolized predictions across hepatocyte systems.

Differences in metabolite representation, inhibitor behavior, and effective inhibitor exposure may influence fm predictions.
