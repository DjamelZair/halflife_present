Recommended Phase 2 Dose for Abemaciclib in Combination With Temozolomide in Pediatric and Young Adult Patients With Relapsed/Refractory Solid Tumors: Results From Study JPCS Part B.

Cyclin-dependent kinase 4/6 (CDK4/6) pathway aberrations, including CDK4 or CCND1 amplification, CDKN2A/B loss, retinoblastoma (RB) dysregulation, and cyclin D overexpression, are relevant for pediatric cancers. Abemaciclib, a selective CDK4/6 inhibitor with preferential CDK4 activity and central nervous system (CNS) penetration, induces sustained G1 arrest.

The recommended phase 2 dose for the combination of abemaciclib and temozolomide is established, showing preliminary antitumor activity.

Steady-state abemaciclib concentrations align with adult efficacy-associated ranges.

A literature-informed pharmacokinetic framework for polymyxin B dosing during continuous renal replacement therapy: integrating renal function and extracorporeal clearance.

Existing population pharmacokinetic (PopPK) models for polymyxin B in patients receiving CRRT remain limited, and all of them have neglected the impact of membrane materials on drug adsorption. Recently, a few studies have attempted to construct integrated PK models by combining pharmacokinetic (PK) parameters derived from patients not receiving CRRT with CRRT-specific parameters.

This study proposes a framework to predict polymyxin B exposure during continuous renal replacement therapy (CRRT), taking into account filter type and residual renal function.

The framework incorporates intrinsic clearance adjusted by CrCL, transmembrane clearance, and adsorptive clearance components.
