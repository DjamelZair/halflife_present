Model-Informed Evaluation of Drug-Drug Interaction Recommendations for Amlodipine and Nirmatrelvir-Ritonavir: A Hypothesis-Generating Analysis.

While nirmatrelvir-ritonavir effectively prevents severe COVID-19, its ritonavir component causes significant drug-drug interactions (DDIs). Current guidelines suggest mitigating this interaction by halving the amlodipine dose or moving to every-other-day (EOD) dosing, then resuming the standard regimen 3-5 days post-antiviral treatment.

Drug-Drug Interaction Assessment of WXFL10203614 With Rifampicin, Itraconazole and Metformin: A Three-Cohort Phase I Study.

To evaluate the effects of rifampicin and itraconazole on the pharmacokinetics (PK) of the Janus kinase 1 (JAK1) inhibitor WXFL10203614 and the effects of steady-state WXFL10203614 on metformin PK and safety.
