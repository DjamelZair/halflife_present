Practical Considerations for the Design, Conduct, and Reporting of Clinical Pharmacokinetic Drug-Drug Interaction Studies in Drug Development.

Clinical pharmacokinetic drug-drug interaction (PK-DDI) studies are frequently required in drug development when metabolizing enzymes and transporters determine pharmacokinetics. Drugs can be victims, perpetrators, or victims and perpetrators, of PK-DDIs, so the clinical consequences of these interactions, particularly the risks of severe adverse effects and therapeutic failures leading to poor clinical outcomes, must be well understood prior to regulatory filings.

Evaluation of Therapeutic Drug Monitoring for Vancomycin with Ratio of Area Under the Curve to Minimum Inhibitory Concentration at a Children's and Women's Tertiary Hospital.

Guidelines for therapeutic drug monitoring (TDM) of vancomycin recommend monitoring the ratio of area under the curve to minimum inhibitory concentration (AUC/MIC), with a target of 400-600 for both adults and children, rather than trough-based monitoring. AUC/MIC-based TDM for vancomycin was implemented at the study centre in April 2021.
