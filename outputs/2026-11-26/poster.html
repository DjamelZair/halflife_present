Clozapine Intoxication: What Can We Learn from Case Reports? A Systematic Review.

Clozapine remains the gold standard for treatment resistant schizophrenia; however, the complex pharmacokinetic and pharmacodynamic properties makes clozapine susceptible to interactions and thus increase the risk of intoxications. A Dynamic, Inflammation-Aware Framework for Safe Clozapine Prescribing in East Asian Populations.

Background: Clozapine is the most effective antipsychotic for treatment-resistant schizophrenia, yet it remains underused in many East Asian settings. Lower average dose requirements have often been attributed to CYP1A2 genetic variation, but genotype alone does not adequately explain interindividual or within-patient variability.

Cytokine-mediated CYP1A2 suppression may cause concentration increases during inflammation

Clozapine exposure reflects both stable and time-varying factors, including smoking, interacting medicines, adherence, and inflammatory illness.
