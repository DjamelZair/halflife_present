Machine learning prediction of cyclosporine nephrotoxicity in pediatric patients using therapeutic drug monitoring and clinical data.

Cyclosporine A (CsA) nephrotoxicity is a dose-limiting toxicity in pediatric immunosuppression, and early identification of children at risk of CsA-associated acute kidney injury (AKI) remains an unmet clinical need. In retrospective cohorts of CsA-treated inpatients undergoing therapeutic drug monitoring (352 children; 342 adults), a regularized logistic-regression model was compared with six machine-learning classifiers under five feature-selection schemes, three small-sample strategies were evaluated, and adult-child transferability was assessed on 95 aligned features.

A machine learning model can predict the risk of ciclosporin-associated acute kidney injury in pediatric patients.

The model uses therapeutic drug monitoring and clinical data, including 14 variables.

Model-based exploration of tamoxifen therapy: addressing exposure and the influence of albumin on proposed endoxifen threshold concentration levels.

Tamoxifen is a cornerstone in the treatment of estrogen receptor-positive breast cancer. While the standard dose of 20 mg/day is widely accepted, emerging evidence suggests that this regimen may not be sufficient to consistently achieve therapeutic endoxifen concentrations in many patients.
