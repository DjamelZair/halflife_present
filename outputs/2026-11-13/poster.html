Physiologically Based Pharmacokinetic Modeling of Fruquintinib and Its Metabolite to Assess Drug-Drug Interaction Liability as an Object or Precipitant.

Fruquintinib is approved, including in the United States and Europe, for previously treated metastatic colorectal cancer, regardless of biomarker status. Using in vitro and clinical data, physiologically based pharmacokinetic (PBPK) models were developed for fruquintinib and M11, a major metabolite of fruquintinib.

Pharmacokinetic determinants, clinical efficacy, and safety of teicoplanin in adults: a correlative analysis of plasma concentration and treatment outcomes.

The aim of this study was to develop and validate a high-performance liquid chromatography (HPLC) method for the determination of plasma teicoplanin concentrations, identify factors influencing teicoplanin exposure in adult patients, and assess its clinical efficacy and safety. A cohort of 125 adult patients who received teicoplanin therapy at the Second Hospital of Hebei Medical University between June 2022 and March 2023 were retrospectively enrolled.
