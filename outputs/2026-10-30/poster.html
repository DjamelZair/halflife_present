Predicting drug-drug interaction and optimal dosing regimens of rivaroxaban combined with ceritinib using physiologically based pharmacokinetic and pharmacodynamic modeling.

Direct oral anticoagulants like rivaroxaban are globally recommended for cancer-associated thrombosis. However, co-administration with the targeted ALK inhibitor ceritinib in lung cancer patients poses a critical risk of drug-drug interactions (DDI) due to potent CYP3A4 inhibition, lacking quantitative clinical assessments.

Pharmacokinetics and Therapeutic Drug Monitoring in Liver and Kidney Disease.

Hepatic and renal dysfunction can substantially alter drug pharmacokinetics. This can lead to unpredictable changes in drug clearance and elimination, which may increase the risk of toxicity or treatment failure.

RNA-encoded PGT121-LS anti-HIV antibody: Comprehensive preclinical characterization and translational pharmacokinetics.

Human immunodeficiency virus (HIV)-1 broadly neutralizing antibodies (bNAbs) have demonstrated clinical efficacy but face manufacturing challenges associated with recombinant protein production and purification. Here, we present a ribonucleic acid (RNA)-encoded bNAb (RibobNAb) platform that enables antibody production of the clinically validated bNAb PGT121 via lipid nanoparticle (LNP) delivery.
