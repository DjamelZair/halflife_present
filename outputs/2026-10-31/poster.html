From CYP450 pharmacogenomics to personalized medicine: clinical evidence, dosing guidelines, and future perspectives.

The CYP450 superfamily mediates the oxidative biotransformation of 70-80% of clinically prescribed medications.

Interaction of the day: ivabradine and isavuconazole

  • isavuconazole is a moderate inhibitor of CYP3A and ivabradine is a sensitive substrate of CYP3A, so ivabradine exposure can be higher.

Illustrative AUC ratio 4.0x (moderate increase), using textbook values that reproduce the FDA category definitions, not values for these exact drugs.
