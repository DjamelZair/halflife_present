What happens every morning

  1. 1

    Collect

    PubMed, bioRxiv, medRxiv, ClinicalTrials.gov, Lareb, EMA, FDA, first in human studies, phase 3 results, and EUDA alerts on new psychoactive substances.

  2. 2

    Pick three

    Two Clinic cards (interactions, PK, TDM, dosing) and one Discovery card (new approvals, new molecules). A new Lareb signal always gets a slot; a EUDA alert becomes a Toxicology card. Nothing repeats.

  3. 3

    Molecule of the day

    The real 3D structure from PubChem, ideally the newest drug from the Discovery card, with its properties worked out by RDKit.

  4. 4

    Interaction of the day

    Shared CYP enzymes from the FDA table and the ICH M12 model, with sliders to play with. European generic names only.

  5. 5

    Push

    GitHub runs it all, saves the day's poster and figures, and sends the notification.
